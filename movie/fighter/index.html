Full Review

Fighter, directed by Siddharth Anand, is a cinematic spectacle that brings the world of aerial warfare to life with remarkable scale and intensity. Starring Hrithik Roshan, Deepika Padukone, and Anil Kapoor, the film presents a fictionalized story inspired by real military events such as the Pulwama attack and the Balakot airstrike. The film follows a team of elite Indian Air Force pilots as they face external threats while dealing with personal struggles and professional responsibilities. Hrithik Roshan delivers a commanding performance as Shamsher "Patty" Pathania, balancing heroism with emotional vulnerability. Anil Kapoor shines as Group Captain "Rocky," bringing authority and depth to his role, while Deepika Padukone adds strength and presence to the ensemble. One of the film’s greatest strengths lies in its aerial action sequences. The dogfights are visually stunning, immersive, and executed with impressive technical precision. Siddharth Anand's direction ensures that the action remains exciting while maintaining an emotional connection to the characters and their mission. The film’s patriotic themes resonate strongly, highlighting the courage, sacrifice, and dedication of the Indian Air Force. Vishal-Shekhar’s music and background score elevate key moments, enhancing both the emotional and action-driven scenes. As a viewer, the movie evokes a strong sense of pride and admiration for the armed forces. While certain elements are dramatized for cinematic impact, the overall message of valor, resilience, and national service remains powerful throughout the film.

What I loved

  • Stunning aerial combat and flight sequences
  • Hrithik Roshan's charismatic performance as Patty
  • Strong supporting performance by Anil Kapoor
  • Patriotic and emotionally engaging storyline
  • High production values and visual effects
  • Powerful climax and covert mission sequence
  • Excellent background score and music by Vishal-Shekhar
  • Strong depiction of camaraderie among Air Force pilots

What I didn't like

  • Some portions are heavily dramatized
  • Predictable narrative beats in certain scenes
  • Focuses more on spectacle than character depth at times

Final Verdict

Fighter is a thrilling and emotionally charged aerial action film that successfully combines patriotism, spectacle, and strong performances. With breathtaking visuals, engaging action sequences, and a heartfelt tribute to India's defenders, it delivers an entertaining and memorable cinematic experience.
