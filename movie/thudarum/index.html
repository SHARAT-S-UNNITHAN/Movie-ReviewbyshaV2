Full Review

Thudarum, directed by Tharun Moorthy and starring Mohanlal and Shobana, is a gripping Malayalam crime thriller that brings both emotional depth and intense suspense to the big screen. Released on 25 April 2025, the film has garnered widespread critical acclaim. The story follows Shanmugham ("Benz"), a humble taxi driver whose cherished vintage Ambassador is wrongfully seized due to a false narcotics charge. His struggle to reclaim the vehicle becomes a powerful journey of justice, resilience, and personal sacrifice. Mohanlal delivers one of his finest performances in recent years, portraying a man battling loss, injustice, and overwhelming circumstances. Shobana provides strong emotional support as Lalitha, while Prakash Varma effectively portrays the antagonist. The supporting cast contributes meaningfully to the film’s emotional and dramatic impact. Tharun Moorthy’s direction and the screenplay co-written with K. R. Sunil keep the audience invested throughout. The film balances emotional family moments with tense dramatic sequences and explores social issues with nuance. Strong cinematography and Jakes Bejoy’s evocative score further enhance the viewing experience. The pacing remains steady despite the lengthy runtime, and the final act leaves a lasting impression. One of the most compelling aspects of the film is its ability to challenge audience assumptions. What initially appears to be a story about a father committing a terrible act gradually reveals deeper truths about coercion, family protection, and moral conflict. The film also addresses crimes committed under the influence of caste, religion, and social status, highlighting the tragic consequences of systemic injustice.

What I loved

  • Mohanlal’s outstanding performance as Shanmugham
  • Strong chemistry between Mohanlal and Shobana
  • Original and emotionally layered storytelling
  • Engaging screenplay with suspense and realism
  • Effective social commentary on caste, religion, and status-based injustice
  • Beautiful cinematography
  • Excellent background score by Jakes Bejoy
  • Well-paced editing and impactful climax

What I didn't like

  • Runtime may feel slightly long for some viewers
  • No major flaws highlighted in the review

Final Verdict

Thudarum is a powerful and emotionally resonant crime thriller that combines suspense, social commentary, and exceptional performances. With strong direction, a compelling screenplay, and a memorable lead performance from Mohanlal, it stands out as one of the best Malayalam films of 2025.
